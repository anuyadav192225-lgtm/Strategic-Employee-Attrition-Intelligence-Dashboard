Strategic Employee Attrition Intelligence Dashboard

📊 Project Overview

The Strategic Employee Attrition Intelligence Dashboard is a Power BI project designed to analyze employee attrition and identify the key factors contributing to employee turnover.

The dashboard transforms employee data into meaningful visual insights that help HR teams understand attrition patterns and make data-driven workforce decisions.

🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall employee attrition.
- Identify departments and job roles with high attrition.
- Understand the impact of age, gender, salary, and experience on attrition.
- Analyze employee satisfaction and work-related factors.
- Identify trends and patterns in employee turnover.
- Support HR teams in developing effective employee retention strategies.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
- DAX – Measures and calculated columns
- Data Visualization
- Excel/CSV – Source data

📌 Key Dashboard Insights

The dashboard includes analysis of:

- Total Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Employee Age
- Average Salary
- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Job Role
- Attrition by Gender
- Attrition by Age Group
- Attrition by Salary/Income
- Attrition by Years of Experience
- Employee Satisfaction and Work Environment

📈 Dashboard Features

- Interactive Power BI visuals
- KPI cards for important HR metrics
- Department and job-role analysis
- Filters and slicers for detailed exploration
- Data-driven insights for employee retention
- Easy-to-understand HR analytics dashboard

💡 Business Value

This project helps organizations identify high-risk attrition areas, understand employee turnover patterns, and support HR teams in making better workforce planning and retention decisions.
